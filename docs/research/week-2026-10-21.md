[F73](facts.md#f73) checks the candidate that adds array and hash mutation sites as writers to F72's rule.
It admits the witness values of 52 of F72's 56 losing rows. Still, 21 rows lose real values, 17 of them new:
13 through routing or missing contributions, 6 in container contents and 2 others. Its equality gains are not
sound gains, and the sampled census bounds the loss rate only loosely.

[F74](facts.md#f74) names the cause of Mastodon's remaining schedule variance: a reader of absence inside a cycle.
A class guard turned a not-yet-computed argument into a Hash, and a six-slot cycle kept that premature value in
some schedules only. With Pending kept pending at the guard, all ten Mastodon tables agree: on F73's candidate
across four schedules, and on a port to upstream main across twelve. The change costs precision in 24 rows and
leaves F73's losses and twelve structural identities open.
